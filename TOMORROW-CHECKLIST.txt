KHALED: THE GAME - checklist for the presentation (Tuesday, Teams)

BEFORE THE MEETING
1. Laptop on power. Close heavy apps (other browsers, OneDrive sync, games).
2. Cable internet, or sit close to the Wi-Fi router.
3. Double-click START-HERE.bat. The game opens in the browser.
4. Press F for full screen.
5. Optional: press P to open your presenter window (notes + timer) on a second screen.

WHEN YOU SHARE IN TEAMS
6. Share -> Window -> the browser window with the game (NOT "Screen").
7. Look for "Optimize for video" / "Optimize for motion" in the Teams share bar.
   If you see it, turn it on.
8. Tick "Include sound" only if you want them to hear the game sounds.

IN THE GAME
9. Press L for Teams mode if the test tonight looked choppy.
10. After each click, pause a moment: viewers see everything 1-2 seconds later.

KEYS
Right arrow / Space / clicker = next      Left arrow = back
M = jump to any world (for questions)     T = hide/show timer
N = notes on screen                       P = presenter window
L = Teams mode                            S = sound on/off
F = full screen                           H = hide the small control buttons
